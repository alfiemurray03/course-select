1. The subscription service

A Learning Library subscription gives each authorised named learner online access to the Sousa Murray courses included in the selected plan while the subscription remains active. Courses may contain modules, lessons, activities, knowledge checks and a final assessment.

The Learning Library is separate from Highfield Professional Training. Highfield courses, regulated qualifications, practical assessments and separately priced professional course licences are not included unless a product page expressly says otherwise.

The Learning Library provides awareness, knowledge and personal-development training. It does not by itself certify workplace competence, grant a professional licence or replace organisation-specific procedures, supervision or specialist advice.

3. Contract formation and payment

You must sign in using JA Group Services ID before purchasing. Stripe processes the payment and creates the recurring billing agreement. A contract is formed when Stripe confirms the successful subscription payment and Sousa Murray eLearning activates the corresponding entitlement.

You authorise JA Group Services Ltd, through Stripe, to charge the selected monthly price at sign-up and on each monthly renewal date until the subscription is cancelled.

You must use a payment method that you are authorised to use and provide accurate billing and account information. We may refuse or reverse activation where payment is unsuccessful, fraudulent, duplicated, unauthorised or affected by an obvious pricing error.

4. Monthly renewal and payment failure

The subscription continues month to month and renews automatically unless cancelled. Stripe normally charges the saved payment method on the monthly renewal date. Stripe invoices and receipts form part of the durable record of the transaction.

If a renewal payment fails, Stripe may retry the payment and contact the customer. We may allow a limited grace period while payment is corrected. Access may then be suspended or ended if payment remains unpaid. Suspension does not erase valid historic completion records or certificates.

We will not impose an early-termination fee because these are monthly plans rather than annual contracts billed monthly.

5. Consumer cooling-off cancellation

If you purchase as a consumer, you normally have 14 days from the day after the subscription contract is made to cancel without giving a reason, subject to the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013 and any other mandatory rights applying at the time.

By asking for immediate Learning Library access during that period, you expressly request that the digital service begins before the cooling-off period ends. If you cancel after access or learning has begun, we may make a fair proportionate deduction for the service supplied before cancellation where the law permits. We will not exclude or restrict a mandatory statutory right.

To exercise a cooling-off right, use the billing portal where available or make a clear statement to contact@jagroupservices.co.uk. Include the account email and enough information to locate the subscription. Refunds that are legally due will be returned without undue delay to the original payment method.

Organisation and business customers: statutory consumer cooling-off rights may not apply where the customer is acting wholly or mainly for business purposes. Any contractual cancellation rights below still apply.

6. Ordinary cancellation after the cooling-off period

You may cancel at any time through the clearly labelled “Manage billing” option in the LMS dashboard and Stripe Customer Portal, or by contacting Sousa Murray eLearning.

Unless mandatory law requires a different result, ordinary cancellation takes effect at the end of the already-paid monthly billing period. No further monthly payment will be taken after the effective cancellation date. Access normally continues until the end of that paid period.

We do not normally provide a pro-rata refund merely because a customer chooses not to use the remaining part of an already-started monthly period. This does not affect refunds or remedies for a faulty, misdescribed or unavailable service, an incorrect charge, a valid cooling-off cancellation, or any other right that cannot lawfully be excluded.

7. Access, availability and suspension

Course access depends on an active entitlement, a supported device and browser, internet access and a working JA Group Services ID. Planned maintenance, urgent security work, third-party failures and circumstances outside reasonable control may temporarily affect availability.

We may suspend an account or learner seat where reasonably necessary to protect security, investigate fraud, enforce a seat limit, respond to unlawful or abusive conduct, protect course integrity or comply with law. We will act proportionately and explain the reason where it is lawful and appropriate to do so.

8. Learning, progress and assessment rules

  • The named learner must complete their own lessons, knowledge checks and final assessments.
  • Lesson progress is recorded centrally and may be required before the final assessment unlocks.
  • The published pass mark applies to the final assessment. A learner who does not pass may review the course and make another attempt unless access has ended or an integrity restriction applies.
  • Automated systems or another person must not be used to answer an assessment on the learner’s behalf.
  • Course content, questions and answers must not be copied, recorded, redistributed, sold, scraped or used to create competing material.

9. Certificates and verification

A completion certificate is issued only after the learner completes the required lessons and passes the final assessment. It records the learner name, course title and code, version, issue date, score and certificate number.

Certificates may have a public verification token so a recipient can check whether the record is valid, withdrawn or superseded. Verification displays limited certificate information and does not expose the learner’s full account or lesson history.

We may correct, supersede or withdraw a certificate where it was issued because of error, fraud, impersonation, assessment misconduct or inaccurate identity information. A fair investigation and appropriate record will be maintained.

10. Team and organisation plans

The organisation owner controls learner invitations and must only invite adults whom it is authorised to enrol. The owner is responsible for keeping within the plan’s named-seat limit and for removing access that is no longer authorised.

An invitation must be accepted using the JA Group Services ID email address to which it was issued. The organisation may see appropriate learner-assignment, progress, completion and certificate information for legitimate training administration. Organisation customers must tell learners about that monitoring and have an appropriate lawful basis.

Further information about rights, lawful bases, international transfers and complaints is in the main Privacy Policy.

12. Retention and certificate verification

Active subscription and learning records are kept for as long as needed to operate the service. Billing and transaction records may be retained for legal, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention and dispute purposes.

Completion and certificate-verification records may be retained after the subscription ends so the learner or a third party can verify a genuine certificate and so we can investigate misuse. We will retain no more information than reasonably necessary for that purpose and apply a documented retention schedule.

Where a proposed material change disadvantages a consumer, the customer may cancel before the change takes effect. Mandatory notice and cancellation rights applying at the time will be honoured.
